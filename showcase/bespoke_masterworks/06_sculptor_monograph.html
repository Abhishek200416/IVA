MONOLITH VI: SILENT BEDROCK

Carved by hand from a single 6-tonne seam of Silurian limestone quarried on Gotland's northern coast. Lindqvist rejects mechanical CNC milling; the concave surfaces bear the rhythmic strikes of 18mm diamond-tipped cold chisels over seven months of solitary excavation.
